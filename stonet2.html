— stone towers II —

Stone Towers II — complete reimagining on a new 640×480 engine, developed 1998–1999 but never released. Third-person RPG with the original character artwork (CEL sprites, 320×200 tiles), 3D dungeon backgrounds, and a new map system. Web port based on the original Pascal source (453x.pas, LOST9.PAS).
