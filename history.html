— development timeline —

1992
First prototype
The earliest surviving builds. An animated game sequence, outro, and raycasting engine already exist by December — suggesting the core DDA renderer and game loop were functional from the very start.
GAME6.FLI   ANIMACE2.FLI   GM_OUT.FLI
1995
The Adventure — main development
The game's definitive year. 129 GIF87a scenes are painted at 320×200 and form the complete dungeon artwork for The Adventure. The story manuscript STONETOW.ERS is written — a Czech poem about the land of Torwen, sorcerer Ragon, six monks, and three lost crystals — signed (c) 1995 WIZ. BMP sprite sheets for monsters, items, and characters are finalized. STONET.EXE is compiled.
STONET/ (129 GIFs)   STONETOW.ERS   BMP/*.BMP   GM_FLI2.FLI
1996
Engine iteration & 3D experiments
162 Pascal source files in PAS/ (versions 73–264) document active, step-by-step iteration of both the rendering engine and game logic. Separately, the lostGFX graphics library for 640×480×256 VGA is written, alongside an early 3D board prototype — the first sign of what will become Stone Towers II.
PAS/ (162 files, ver. 73–264)   stonet2/3DDESKA0.GIF
1997
Demo & new FX animations
New animated effects — a looping crystal and a water sequence — are produced for planned dungeon scenes in WIZ/NEW10 and WIZ/NEW11. In March, a game demo animation is recorded: the last known stonet1 artifact, suggesting a public demo was planned but never shipped.
CRYSTAL.FLI   STONWAT.FLI   DEMO.FLI   stonet2/3DDESKA1.GIF
1998–99
Stone Towers II — new engine
Development restarts from scratch. A new engine targets 640×480 with CEL-format sprites (64 KB each). Seven distinct executable builds survive (230X through 453X). A full set of character portraits and game screens is painted in GIF. An AVI clip (ANIMACKA.AVI, 1.6 MB) is recorded. The project ends unreleased in early 1999.
stonet2/*.GIF   stonet2/ANIMACKA.AVI   stonet2/*.EXE (7 builds)
2025
Web port — published with authors' permission
The original source code is archived on GitHub. Three web ports are published and the archive is made accessible in the browser — with the assistance of Claude AI — completing the gameplay that was never finished.

— stone towers II —

Stone Towers II is a completely independent reimagining begun in late 1998 — nearly two years after stonet1 development ended. It used an entirely new engine targeting 640×480 in 256-color mode, with CEL-format sprites (64 KB each) replacing the GIF-based scene system of the original.

Seven distinct executable builds survive in the archive, version-numbered 230X through 453X, documenting active iteration through early 1999. The new artwork — four character selection portraits and a set of game screens — shows the visual direction the sequel was taking. An AVI clip (ANIMACKA.AVI) was recorded, likely as a cutscene or intro. The project was never released.
